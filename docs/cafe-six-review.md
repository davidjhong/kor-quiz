# Six-question café content review

Source: `content/cafe-six.json`. Correct answers and explanations below are for creator review; question 6 is not revealed in the video.

Difficulty is relative to English-speaking beginners: basic phrase recognition, then situation matching, then distinguishing plausible requests in context. These labels are editorial judgments, not measured learner outcomes.

Romanization follows the established [Revised Romanization convention](https://www.korean.go.kr/front_eng/roman/roman_01.do). Spellings retain Korean word spacing and are pronunciation aids rather than exact phonetic transcriptions. Review naturalness and pronunciation independently; schema validation does not prove either. In particular, review the pragmatic distinction in the bonus between 로 (the replacement choice), 도 (also), and 더 (more). `masilgeyo` retains the established convention rather than a pronunciation-style `kk`.

## 1. cafe-request-water — EASY

Scenario: At the counter. Context: Ask the café staff politely.

Prompt: **You need water. What do you say?**

| Choice | Korean | Romanization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 물 있어요. | mul isseoyo. |
| B | 물 주세요. | mul juseyo. |
| C | 물 마셔요. | mul masyeoyo. |

Correct: **B — 물 주세요.**. Meaning: **Water, please.**

Explanation: 물 주세요 means “Water, please.” 주세요 politely asks someone to give you something.

Short teaching point: 주세요 = please give me

## 2. cafe-ask-price — EASY

Scenario: Paying. Context: You are ready to pay for your drink.

Prompt: **Ask how much your drink costs.**

| Choice | Korean | Romanization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 얼마예요? | eolmayeyo? |
| B | 괜찮아요. | gwaenchanayo. |
| C | 감사합니다. | gamsahamnida. |

Correct: **A — 얼마예요?**. Meaning: **How much is it?**

Explanation: 얼마예요? means “How much is it?” 얼마 asks about price. 감사합니다 means “Thank you.”

Short teaching point: 얼마 asks about the price.

## 3. cafe-takeaway — MEDIUM

Scenario: Choosing takeaway. Context: You are leaving the café.

Prompt: **You want your drink to go. What do you say?**

| Choice | Korean | Romanization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 여기서 마실게요. | yeogiseo masilgeyo. |
| B | 다 마셨어요. | da masyeosseoyo. |
| C | 포장해 주세요. | pojanghae juseyo. |

Correct: **C — 포장해 주세요.**. Meaning: **To go, please.**

Explanation: 포장해 주세요 asks for takeaway. 여기서 마실게요 means “I will drink it here,” which does not fit leaving the café.

Short teaching point: 포장 asks for takeaway; 여기서 means here.

## 4. cafe-without-ice — MEDIUM

Scenario: Customizing a drink. Context: Ordering a cold drink.

Prompt: **Order your drink without ice. What do you say?**

| Choice | Korean | Romanization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 얼음 빼 주세요. | eoreum ppae juseyo. |
| B | 얼음 더 주세요. | eoreum deo juseyo. |
| C | 얼음 넣어 주세요. | eoreum neoeo juseyo. |

Correct: **A — 얼음 빼 주세요.**. Meaning: **No ice, please.**

Explanation: 얼음 빼 주세요 means “No ice, please.” 빼 주세요 asks to leave something out; 더 주세요 asks for more.

Short teaching point: 빼 주세요 = leave it out; 더 주세요 = give more

## 5. cafe-replace-wrong-drink — HARD

Scenario: Fixing an order. Context: You ordered tea, but received coffee.

Prompt: **Ask to replace the coffee with tea.**

| Choice | Korean | Romanization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 커피로 바꿔 주세요. | keopiro bakkwo juseyo. |
| B | 차로 바꿔 주세요. | charo bakkwo juseyo. |
| C | 차 한 잔 더 주세요. | cha han jan deo juseyo. |

Correct: **B — 차로 바꿔 주세요.**. Meaning: **Please change it to tea.**

Explanation: 차로 바꿔 주세요 asks to replace it with tea. 커피로 changes it to coffee; 차 한 잔 더 주세요 asks for an extra cup of tea.

Short teaching point: 차로 = to tea; 더 asks for more.

## 6. cafe-sold-out-alternative — HARD

Scenario: Choosing an alternative. Context: Your first choice is sold out. No drink is served.

Prompt: **Ask for an Americano instead.**

| Choice | Korean | Romanization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 아메리카노도 주세요. | amerikanodo juseyo. |
| B | 아메리카노 더 주세요. | amerikano deo juseyo. |
| C | 아메리카노로 주세요. | amerikanoro juseyo. |

Correct: **C — 아메리카노로 주세요.**. Meaning: **An Americano instead, please.**

Explanation: 로 marks the replacement choice. 도 means “also”; 더 means “more.” Here you are switching your order, not adding another drink.

Short teaching point: 로 marks the new choice; 도 = also; 더 = more.

Suggested manual comment: C: 아메리카노로 주세요. = An Americano instead, please. 로 marks the replacement; 도 means also and 더 means more.

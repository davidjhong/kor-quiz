The creator describes this as a subtle, looping bass tune and tags it as ambient,
calming music. The source and hashes are preserved in public/audio/music/manifest.json.
CC0 permits copying, modification and commercial use; it provides no warranty of
ownership or third-party rights. The source declaration, rather than a generic
“royalty-free” label, is the basis for selecting it.

Local preparation normalizes to −20 LUFS / −3 dBTP, stereo 48 kHz, and crossfades
250 ms of the tail/head into a continuous loop. Rendering applies quiet gain,
additional speech ducking, quieter countdown gain and one-second boundary fades.
No music service or song-generation API is used. Set backgroundAudio to false
in the comparison presentation configuration to disable it.
